Questions, answered

How does a masterclass work?

Each masterclass is a live 1-day session on Google Meet or Zoom, followed by a Q&A and a take-home guide you can apply right away.

Do I get a customized diet?

Yes — every nutrition plan is built around your preferences, schedule and goals, not a fixed meal template.

How do I book a seat?

Tap "Book Now" on any course card, message us on WhatsApp, or call +91 91525 59585 — seats are limited per batch.

Can beginners join?

Absolutely. Every masterclass is built from your current starting point, whether that's day one or year ten.

Do I need supplements?

No. Supplements are optional and only ever recommended where they genuinely fill a gap in your diet.
